SEVA.GIS: free satellite crop monitoring

SEVA.GIS (Spatial Evaluation & Vegetation Analytics) turns your farm boundary into live Sentinel-2 crop health maps. See where your crop is stressed, whether to irrigate, and where to apply fertilizer, free and with no account.

  • Crop health indices: NDVI, NDMI, NDWI, NDRE, EVI and BSI
  • Terrain, water, weather and soil information for your field
  • Tractor driving paths and variable-rate fertilizer zones
  • Printable field report in English, Hindi and Telugu
