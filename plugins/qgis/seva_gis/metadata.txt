about=SEVA.GIS (Spatial Evaluation & Vegetation Analytics) turns sentences such as "NDVI", "true colour", "danger zones", "streams where water flows" or "make a red boundary line" into real layers on your QGIS map, in the order you arranged them. Boxes can be added with +, switched off, reordered by dragging or with the up and down arrows, and every layer arrives with its source, dates, resolution and limitations attached. Free and open to everyone: research scholars, GIS analysts, surveyors, agricultural officers, hydrologists and anyone curious about a piece of land.
