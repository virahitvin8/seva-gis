Methodology · browser first

From boundary to evidence, without hiding the seams.

SEVA.GIS keeps the data path legible: what is drawn locally, what is requested from open services, and what is computed before a number reaches the screen.

01 / INPUT

Draw or import the AOI

Delineate a field in EPSG:4326 or import GeoJSON, KML or zipped Shapefile. Validate topology and compute area in a projected CRS, never in degrees.

EPSG:4326 · UTM AREA
02 / COMPUTE

Read open satellite pixels

STAC discovery finds recent scenes. Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF range requests read the needed bands in the browser. SCL masking removes clouds, cirrus and shadows.

STAC · COG · SCL
03 / OUTPUT

Turn evidence into decisions

Compute indices, compare timestamps and export maps and reports. Stress markers remain candidates until a field visit confirms them.

MAPS · REPORTS · GIS EXPORT

Scientific guardrails

No silent zeros. No invented certainty.

Failure states are part of the interface. The system tells you what is missing, why, and what to do next.

Valid geometry

Area is computed in a projected CRS and the polygon is persisted in WGS84.

Cloud-masked

Masked pixels are excluded from statistics and identified in the quality note.

Unavailable

If a provider times out or returns no scene, show the technical reason and retry. Never substitute zero.

Privacy & data flow

Know exactly what leaves your device.

Stays in your browser

AOI geometry, index computation, cloud masking, charts and the local workspace store remain on your device in the public app.

Leaves your device when needed

Scene discovery requests include your AOI bounding box and observation window. Weather and soil services receive the required point or area query. Optional backend or Google Earth Engine processing activates only when a deployment configures it.

This prototype

This static entry screen sends no form data. The sign-in action validates locally and reports that no credentials were transmitted.
